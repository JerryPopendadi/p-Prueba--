01 —Philosophy

Beauty begins with inner harmony.

The Faces is a space of love, where each element comes to life within you. We believe beauty is more than appearance: it is a deep connection with yourself and the world around you.

It started with the five senses (sight, hearing, smell, touch and taste) and a sixth one, intuition, the one that brought you here. Ayurveda then gave us the five elements. And just as the senses have intuition, the elements have a force that unites them all: love.

Silence, temperature, light, textures, fragrance and the therapist's movements become one ritual. Every visit begins long before the first touch.

Anna & Evgenii Dubinin · Founders
