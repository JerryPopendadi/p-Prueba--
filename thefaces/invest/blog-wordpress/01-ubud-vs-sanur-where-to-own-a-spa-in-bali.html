Ubud and Sanur are only 45 minutes apart, yet they attract different guests, run on different rhythms and suit different spa formats. If you are thinking about owning a share of a spa in Bali, the location will shape your guest flow, your average ticket and the kind of team you need. This guide compares the two areas and the four THE FACES projects currently open to partners.

Why Bali for a spa business

Bali welcomed 6.95 million foreign visitors in 2025, 9.7% more than in 2024 (BPS-Statistics Indonesia, Bali Province). Wellness is part of why many of them come: the Global Wellness Institute values the global wellness economy at US$6.8 trillion in 2024, with a forecast close to US$9.8 trillion by 2029. These figures describe the market, not the results of any single spa, but they explain why well-run spas on the island stay busy.

Ubud: the wellness heart of the island

Ubud is where travellers go for yoga, retreats, jungle views and slow days. Guests often stay several nights and look for longer rituals rather than a quick massage.

  • Guests: wellness travellers, digital nomads, long-stay visitors and residents.
  • What sells: signature rituals, face and head treatments, nature-led experiences.
  • Strength: a destination people choose on purpose, with demand all year.
  • Watch out for: strong competition, so the concept must be truly distinctive.

Sanur: beachfront flow and a premium ticket

Sanur is a calm beach town with big hotels, families and returning visitors. Foot traffic along the main streets is high, which lowers the cost of bringing guests in.

  • Guests: hotel guests, couples, families, repeat visitors and residents.
  • What sells: fast, high-quality treatments for passers-by and premium rituals for couples.
  • Strength: street visibility and a steady tourist flow.
  • Watch out for: the premises and the lease decide everything; a good corner is worth more than décor.

  4. How much involvement do you want? In all four, you do not run the spa: a professional local team does.

FAQ

Is Ubud or Sanur better for a spa?

Neither is better in general. Ubud suits signature rituals for wellness travellers; Sanur suits high-visibility formats with steady tourist flow. The project, the premises and the lease matter more than the area alone.

How much does it cost to become a partner in a Bali spa?

The four THE FACES projects currently range from $250,000 to $450,000, depending on the stage and the format.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Profit varies from month to month and is not guaranteed; capital can be lost. Review every document with your own advisers.
