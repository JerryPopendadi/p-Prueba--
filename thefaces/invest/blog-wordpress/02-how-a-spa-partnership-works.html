A spa partnership lets you own part of a real business without running it. You invest in one specific spa, become a shareholder and receive your share of the profit every month, while an experienced team handles everything from therapists to bookings. Here is how the model works at THE FACES, step by step.

The model in four steps

  1. You invest in one specific spa. Not a pooled fund: you know exactly which premises, team and concept your money goes to.
  2. You become a shareholder. Your stake is set out in a written agreement.
  3. THE FACES runs the business. Team, rituals, marketing, bookings and accounting.
  4. You are paid monthly. Your share of the profit, with a monthly financial report.

Typical terms

For most projects the typical structure is up to 49% of shares and up to 50% of profit. For THE FACES SANUR Five Elements the structure is a 50/50 partnership. Everything is agreed individually and in writing, and only where the law of the partner's country allows.

An illustrative example

Imagine a spa with a net profit of $25,000 in a month and a partner entitled to 50% of the profit. That partner would receive $12,500 that month, or $150,000 over a year at the same level. On a $250,000 ticket, that would recover the capital in about 1.7 years.

This is an illustration, not a forecast. Profit changes from month to month with season, occupancy and costs, and it can be lower or zero. That is why real figures are reviewed during due diligence.

How partners are protected

Protection depends on each project and is written into the definitive agreements. For WATERFALL, for example, the proposed terms include a notarised shareholding registered with the Indonesian Ministry of Law (AHU), capital released in tranches against milestones, a veto on key decisions and pre-agreed exit options (right of first refusal, buy-back and tag-along). From first call to agreement

  1. Intro call: 30 minutes with the founders, without obligation.
  2. Investor pack: budget, financial model, lease, permits and schedule.
  3. Due diligence: a site visit in Bali or an online walkthrough, with your own advisers.
  4. Agreement: written terms, shareholding registered where applicable, regular reporting.

FAQ

Do I need to work in the spa?

No. THE FACES runs day-to-day operations. You receive reports and keep the rights set in your agreement.

How often are profits paid?

Monthly, together with a financial report, according to the agreement.

Can I exit later?

Exit and transfer conditions are agreed before you invest. Private shares are illiquid and a buyer is not guaranteed.
