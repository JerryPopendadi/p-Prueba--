Numbers on a screen tell part of the story. Walking into the spa tells the rest. If you are considering a share of a spa in Bali, nothing replaces a visit. Here is a simple three-day plan that combines due diligence with the best of the island.

Day 1 · Ubud: an operating spa and the jungle

  • Morning: book a treatment at THE FACES Ubud as a regular guest. Notice the welcome, the timing, the care.
  • Afternoon: meet the founders and the team. Review the booking data and monthly accounts together.
  • Visit: the WATERFALL construction site, with its stone rooms and arched windows onto the jungle.
  • Evening: the rice terraces at sunset.

Day 2 · Sanur: location, flow and two new projects

  • Morning: walk the street in front of THE FACES SANUR, opposite the Hyatt, at different times of the day. Count the people passing. Look at the 250 m² building and the plans for both floors.
  • Afternoon: visit The White Temple, where renovation is under way.
  • Review: leases (both projects have 10-year leases signed), budgets and schedules.

Day 3 · Uluwatu: time to think

Leave the last day free. Watch the sunset from the cliffs of Uluwatu, read your notes and write down your questions for the founders and your advisers. Good decisions need a quiet mind, and Bali is good at that.

What to bring home

  1. Your own notes on guest flow and service quality.
  2. The investor pack: accounts, booking data, lease, permits, structure.
  3. Your list of questions for your lawyer and tax adviser.
