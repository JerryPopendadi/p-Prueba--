Yes, foreigners can hold a share of a spa business in Bali, but the structure matters. Most foreign partners invest through an Indonesian company with foreign shareholders, known as a PT PMA, or through contractual arrangements reviewed by local counsel. This article explains the main options in plain words. It is general information, not legal advice.

What is a PT PMA?

A PT PMA (Perseroan Terbatas Penanaman Modal Asing) is an Indonesian limited liability company that can have foreign shareholders. It is the standard vehicle for foreign direct investment in Indonesia. A PT PMA is registered with the Ministry of Law and Human Rights, holds a business identification number (NIB) and operates under the business classification (KBLI) of its activity.

Indonesian regulations set minimum investment and capital requirements for PT PMA companies. These rules change over time, so the current figures for your specific project should always be confirmed by an Indonesian lawyer before you commit.

How a partner can hold a share in a spa

  • Shares in the operating company: the partner becomes a registered shareholder. At THE FACES, the proposed structure for projects such as WATERFALL is a notarised shareholding registered with the Ministry of Law (AHU), so the partner is a legal shareholder, not a lender.
  • Shareholder agreement: sets profit share, reporting, reserved matters (veto rights), transfer and exit.
  • Management agreement: defines what the operator does and how it is paid.

What to check before signing

  1. The company's deed of establishment and latest amendments.
  2. The NIB, KBLI and the licences needed for spa activity.
  3. The lease: duration, prepaid period, renewal and transfer clauses.
  4. Who owns the trademark and the treatment concept.
  5. The shareholder agreement: profit share, veto rights, reporting, exit.
  6. Tax: how profits are distributed and taxed in Indonesia and in your country.

Leases are as important as ownership

In Bali, most spas operate on leased premises. A long lease protects the business you are investing in. Two THE FACES projects already have 10-year leases signed: The White Temple and THE FACES SANUR (where the lease is also partly prepaid).

FAQ

Can a foreigner own 100% of a spa in Bali?

It depends on the current investment rules for the activity and the structure chosen. Ask an Indonesian lawyer for the rules that apply to your case.

Do I need to live in Indonesia?

No. Partners can hold shares from abroad; reporting and meetings can be online.

Who signs the documents?

Shareholdings are formalised by notarial deed in Indonesia. Your own lawyer should review every document first.
