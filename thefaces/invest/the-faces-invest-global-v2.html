08 / Investor questions

Before you decide.

What is the minimum investment?

There is no fixed minimum. It depends on the project, its valuation and the share offered. Amounts are shared individually.

How large are development projects?

New development projects discussed so far have had a reference value of roughly US$500,000–600,000*. This is indicative only, not a price or a minimum investment.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. We do not publish or promise returns. Distributions depend on business performance and agreed terms, and capital may be lost.

Do I have to run the spa?

No. The proposed model includes professional local management with regular reporting to investors.

Can foreigners invest in Indonesia?

Usually through a foreign-investment company (PT PMA) or contractual arrangements, reviewed with Indonesian legal counsel.

How do I exit?

Exit and transfer rights are agreed in the shareholder agreement before investing. Private shares are illiquid and a buyer is not guaranteed.

Does a call commit me to anything?

No. The call and the investor pack are free and without obligation.
