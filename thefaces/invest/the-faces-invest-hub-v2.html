08 · FAQ

Before you decide.

How are the terms shared?

Individually, after an introductory call, in writing, and only where the law of your country allows. We do not publish investment amounts or returns.

Who are the projects for?

Hotel groups, developers, property owners and private partners able to commit significant capital to a premium wellness venue.

Do I have to run the spa?

No. THE FACES keeps the brand and the management and runs the spa under a written agreement that sets responsibilities, fees and reporting.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Profit changes from month to month, is not guaranteed and can be zero, and you could lose part or all of your capital. Review every document with your own advisers.

Can a foreigner take part in an Indonesian business?

It depends on Indonesian law, sector rules and the structure — often a foreign-investment company (PT PMA) or contractual arrangements. Get independent legal advice.

Can I visit first?

Yes, please do. Book a treatment in Ubud as a guest, or ask us for a site visit or an online walk-through of the other projects.
