Your protection

How the partnership is structured

Proposed for every partner and written into the final agreements, which your own lawyer reviews.

Registered shares

Formalised by notarial deed and registered with the Indonesian Ministry of Law (AHU). You become a shareholder, not a lender.

Legal for foreigners

Through an Indonesian company with foreign shareholders (PT PMA). You can verify the structure with your own counsel.

Capital in tranches

Released against agreed construction and launch milestones from a dedicated project account.

Monthly reporting

Revenue, costs and bookings every month, with access to the books and bank statements on request.

Veto on key decisions

New debt, sale of assets, changes to the lease or budget need the partner's approval.

Documents first

Lease, permits, budget and schedule are shared for your due diligence before anything is signed.

Why don't you publish amounts or returns?

Terms are shared individually, after a first call, and only where the law of your country allows. Profit varies from month to month and capital can be lost.

Do I need to run the spa?

No. THE FACES manages construction, hiring, training, marketing, daily operations and accounting. You receive monthly reports and vote on key decisions.

Can a foreigner own shares in a Bali business?

Yes, through an Indonesian company with foreign shareholders (PT PMA). We explain the structure on the call; your own lawyer can verify it.

Can I visit before deciding?

Yes. Book a treatment as a guest, meet the team and see the sites in Bali, or join a live video walkthrough.

What happens after I send the form?

We read your request personally and reply, usually within 24 hours, with a link to book a private call with the founders.
