From a first call to signed terms.

  1. Private call

    30 minutes with the founders about your goals and the right format.

A good fit if you…

  • prefer an operating business you can visit to a project on paper
  • live in Bali or want a base here and want to understand its wellness market
  • are a hotel group, developer, property owner or private partner with significant capital
  • accept the risks of a private company and a long horizon

Probably not if you…

  • want guaranteed or short-term returns
  • cannot afford to lose the capital you put in
  • need an asset you can sell quickly
  • are looking for personal financial advice — we do not give it

07 · FAQ

Before you decide.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Returns and distributions are not guaranteed and can be zero. You can lose all the capital you put in.

What are the terms?

They are discussed individually after a first call and set in a written agreement, only where local law allows. The brand, management and the controlling stake stay with the founders. We do not publish amounts or returns.

Do I have to run the spa?

No. Professional local management is part of the model, under a written agreement that sets responsibilities, fees and reporting.

Can a foreigner take part in an Indonesian business?

It depends on Indonesian law, sector rules and the structure — often a foreign-investment company (PT PMA) or contractual arrangements. Get independent legal advice.

Can I ask about the other projects?

Yes. On the same call we can go through The White Temple, WATERFALL and THE FACES SANUR, and which stage suits you.

Can I visit the spa first?

Yes, please do. Book any treatment as a client — it is the best way to understand the business.
