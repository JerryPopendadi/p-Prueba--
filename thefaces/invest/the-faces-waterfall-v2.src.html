Partner protection

Proposed protections for the partner

The structure below is what we propose to every partner. All points are written into the definitive agreements and reviewed by your own lawyer.

Notarised shareholding

Participation is formalised by notarial deed and registered with the Indonesian Ministry of Law (AHU). The partner becomes a legal shareholder, not a lender.

Capital in tranches

Funds are released against agreed construction and launch milestones from a dedicated project account, not paid in one lump sum.

Monthly reporting

Revenue, costs, bookings and payouts every month. Full access to the books and bank statements on request.

Veto on key decisions

Reserved matters such as new debt, sale of assets, change of lease or budget need the partner’s approval.

Documents before you sign

Lease agreement, permits, budget and construction schedule are shared in the data room for your due diligence.

Clear exit options

Right of first refusal, pre-agreed buy-back and tag-along terms, so you know in advance how you can exit.

Protection mechanisms are proposed terms and become binding only once included in the signed shareholder and partnership agreements. Any participation involves risk, including possible loss of capital.

How are participation terms shared?

Individually, after an introductory call, and only where the law of your country allows. We do not publish amounts or returns.

Do I need to be involved in running the spa?

No. THE FACES manages construction, hiring, training, marketing, daily operations and accounting. You receive monthly reports and vote on key decisions.

Can a foreigner own shares in a Bali business?

Yes, through an Indonesian company with foreign shareholders (PT PMA). Our lawyers explain the structure during the intro call; you can verify it with your own counsel.

When does the spa open?

After construction is completed. The launch timeline is shared in the project pack after the introductory call.

How is capital controlled?

Capital is released in tranches against milestones from a dedicated project account, with monthly reporting and access to bank statements.

What are the risks?

Construction delays, slower ramp-up, lower demand, currency movements and regulatory changes. They are discussed openly in the project pack, together with how we manage them.

Can I visit the site?

Yes. We organise site visits in Bali and live video walkthroughs for partners abroad.
