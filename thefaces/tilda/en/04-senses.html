02 —The five senses & intuition

Every ritual speaks to all five senses.

It all began with research into the five senses we use to reach total relaxation. The sixth, intuition, is the one that brought you here.

01
01 / 06

Sight

Soft light, natural textures

Candlelight, wood, stone and woven rattan. Nothing loud for the eyes: the space slows you down before the treatment begins.

02 / 06

Hearing

Singing bowls and silence

Sound therapy with singing bowls in the Air and Love rituals, and real silence afterwards. Vibration calms the nervous system faster than words.

03 / 06

Smell

Flowers, warm oils, herbs

Fresh flowers, warm aromatic oils and herbal pouches. Scent is the shortest way to memory: one breath, and the ritual begins again.

04 / 06

Touch

Hands that know the face

Myofascial, sculptural and buccal techniques, warm water, foam like a cloud. Touch is the heart of The Faces: every master is trained in our signature methods.

05 / 06

Taste

Herbal infusions to close the ritual

Herbal infusions are part of our rituals. A warm cup at the end returns you gently to the day, still inside the calm you found.

06 / 06

Intuition

The sixth sense

The sense that connects us to our deepest self. Trust it to choose your element: how you feel today already knows which ritual you need.
