11 —Questions

Before your visit.

How do I book?

Add treatments to “Your ritual”, choose Ubud or Sanur and pick the master and time online, or send the ritual by WhatsApp. The team confirms within minutes during working hours.

Should I book in advance?

Ideally a day or two ahead, especially for three-hour rituals and evenings. Same-day slots are sometimes available on WhatsApp.

What should I choose for a first visit?

For the face, start with Myofascial or Sculptural face massage. For deep rest, choose Shirodhara Hair Spa or one of the five-element programs.

Do you have treatments for couples and kids?

Yes. Element of Love for two and Sacred Eternal Love are made for couples (Sacred Honeymoon in Sanur), and there is a gentle hair spa for kids.

What currency are the prices in?

All prices are in Indonesian rupiah (IDR). Exact prices and payment options are confirmed when you book.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, according to the salon rules. See the cancellation and rescheduling policies at the bottom of the page.

Do you sell gift certificates?

Yes, with a bonus on top of the amount. Order on WhatsApp; the terms are in the gift certificate policy.
