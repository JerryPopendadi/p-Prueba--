01. Who we are

This website belongs to THE FACES spa salons in Ubud and Sanur, Bali. The data controller is Evgenii Dubinin (THE FACES). For any privacy question or request write to info@thefacespeace.com.

02. What data we collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, phone or e-mail when you write to us on WhatsApp, by e-mail or subscribe to our news. Bookings are made in the Altegio system, which processes your booking data under its own privacy policy.

03. Cookies and analytics

Analytics and advertising cookies (Google, Meta) are used only after you accept them in the cookie banner. If you reject them, the site works normally and no such cookies are set. A small note in your browser remembers your choice and your language.

04. Why we use it

To answer your questions, confirm and manage bookings, send news you asked for, and understand how the website is used so we can improve it. We never sell your data.

05. How long we keep it

Messages and booking data are kept as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting duties. You can unsubscribe from news at any time.

06. Health information

Some treatments require information about allergies, pregnancy, skin sensitivity or health conditions. We ask only for what is needed for a safe treatment, keep it to the staff who need it and never use it for advertising.

07. Partners and business enquiries

If you contact us about a partnership, franchise or a spa in your property, we use the details you send to answer and arrange a meeting. We never ask for bank or card details or passport copies through the website.

08. Third parties and international transfers

We work with hosting and WordPress providers, the Altegio booking system, e-mail and WhatsApp, Google and Meta (only with your consent for advertising) and professional advisers. Data may be processed in Indonesia or other countries with appropriate safeguards.

09. Security

The site uses encrypted connections, restricted administrator access and regular updates. No online system is completely secure; if a data breach happens we will assess it and notify as the law requires.

10. Children

Our services and this website are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Treatments for children (such as the kids hair spa) are booked by a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced on the website.

12. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@thefacespeace.com. We follow Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law No. 27/2022 and, for visitors from the European Union, the GDPR.
