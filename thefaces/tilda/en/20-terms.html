Cancellation

You can cancel a booking in the booking system (Altegio) or by writing to the salon on WhatsApp. Please let us know as early as possible so another guest can take the time. The conditions that apply to your booking are shown when you book and confirmed by the salon.

Rescheduling

To change the day, time or master, write to the salon on WhatsApp or change the booking in Altegio. We will offer you the nearest available time.
