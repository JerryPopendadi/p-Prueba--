A day spa in Sanur for face, hair and body

The Faces Spa Sanur is on Jl. Danau Buyan 34a, in a quiet street of Sanur, the relaxed seaside town on Bali's sunrise coast. Light interiors, soft sand tones and an easy pace: the perfect place to come after the beach or a morning on the promenade.

The Sanur salon is our home for face technology and deep care: Hydrolift, detox hair spa combined with myofascial or sculptural face massage, Aqua Glow Ritual and four-hands treatments. Couples come for Sacred Honeymoon. Every price is published before you book.

Questions about the Sanur salon

What is Hydrolift?

A 60-minute face treatment that works on deep skin layers and facial muscles: less puffiness, better tone and contour. It is only available in Sanur, also combined with a face massage (90 min).

Do you have a ritual for couples in Sanur?

Yes. Sacred Honeymoon (200 min) is our longest ritual for two and is only available in Sanur.

Can I come just before my flight?

Yes. The Before Flight Ritual takes 180 minutes in Sanur; leave enough time to reach the airport afterwards.
