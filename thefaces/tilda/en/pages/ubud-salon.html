A day spa in Ubud for face, head and body

The Faces Spa Ubud is on Jl. Raya Lungsiakan in Kedewatan, the green, quiet side of Ubud on the way to the Ayung river valley. It is a calm place to slow down: woven baskets, natural light and the sound of the jungle instead of traffic.

Here we work with the five elements and love: face massage and sculpting, Japanese-style head spa, skin care and body massage. Only in Ubud you will find Let Your Hair Shine, Lymphatic Glow, the hair spa for kids and the men's programs. Every price is published before you book.

Questions about the Ubud salon

Yes. The Ubud salon is open every day from 10:00 to 22:00. Choose a start time that leaves room for the full length of the treatment.

Yes: Power Reset (130 min) and Stress Free (120 min), both only in Ubud.
